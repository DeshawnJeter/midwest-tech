WHO SAID THAT? — PERSONALIZED EDITION

HOW TO OPEN
Double-click Who-Said-That.html. It opens in your web browser.
No installation, account, server, or internet connection is required.
Use one device together and take turns.

YOUR TABLE — 4 PLAYERS, EXACTLY 15 CARDS EACH
Me: relationships, intelligence, and technology.
Sarah: animals and the South.
Jeter: comics and art.
Eric: hiking and outdoor activities.

The 60 questions and sample answers are already filled in. Answers are
generated examples inspired by the supplied interests, not verified personal
facts or actual quotes. The goal is to match each card to its assigned person.
Use Edit our cards before starting if you want to substitute your real answers.
Each person always has 15 cards; no extra cards are added.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Agree on 1–5 laps and choose one or two dice.
2. Click Play with our 60 questions.
3. Roll. Your piece moves clockwise around the 24-space board.
4. Read the anonymous question and answer. Guess who the card belongs to.
5. Correct: stay on the new space. Incorrect: move back two spaces.
6. Advance to the next player's turn.
7. The first person to finish the agreed laps wins after resolving the question.

You never receive your own card. The deck reshuffles when no unseen cards
remain for the current player. Passing Start backward reduces lap progress.
You cannot go behind your original starting position. Movement on the final
lap stops at the finish. A wrong finish-line guess sends you back two spaces.

CARD EDITING
Choose Edit our cards, select a player, and edit their 15 questions and answers.
Previous and Next save the card. Save card & close returns to setup.
No names need to be typed and no answers are required before the first game.

Your game and edits last while the page stays open. Refreshing or reopening
starts over with the original 60 cards. Nothing is uploaded or shared.
